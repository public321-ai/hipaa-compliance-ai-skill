# Privacy Rule (45 CFR 164.500-534)

## Scope
Use and disclosure of PHI; individual rights; administrative requirements.

## Key Controls
- **Minimum Necessary**: Limit PHI access to minimum necessary to accomplish purpose.
- **Individual Rights**: Access, amendment, accounting of disclosures, restrictions, confidential communications.
- **Authorizations**: Required for uses beyond TPO (Treatment, Payment, Healthcare Operations).
- **Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP)**: Must be provided and acknowledged.
- **Business Associate Agreements (BAA)**: Required before disclosing PHI to BAs.

## Validation Checklist
- [ ] NPP is current and distributed
- [ ] BAAs are executed and reviewed annually
- [ ] Minimum necessary policies enforced technically
- [ ] Individual rights request SLA < 30 days (access) / 60 days (accounting)
- [ ] Workforce training completed annually

## Prohibited Disclosures
- Marketing without authorization
- Sale of PHI without authorization
- Genetic information for underwriting

## Escalation
Any unauthorized disclosure -> trigger `breach_notification.md`
