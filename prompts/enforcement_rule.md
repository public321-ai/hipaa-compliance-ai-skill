# Enforcement Rule (45 CFR 160)

## Penalty Structure
| Tier | Violation | Knowledge | Penalty per Violation | Annual Max |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------------------|------------|
| 1 | Unknown | Did not know | $137 - $68,928 | $2,067,813 |
| 2 | Reasonable cause | Not willful neglect | $1,379 - $68,928 | $2,067,813 |
| 3 | Willful neglect, corrected | Corrected within 30 days | $13,785 - $68,928 | $2,067,813 |
| 4 | Willful neglect, not corrected | Not corrected | $68,928 - $2,067,813 | $2,067,813 |

## State Attorney General Authority
- Can bring civil actions for HIPAA violations
- Penalties: up to $25,000 per violation category per calendar year

## Criminal Penalties
- Wrongful disclosure: up to $50,000 / 1 year imprisonment
- False pretenses: up to $100,000 / 5 years
- Commercial gain or malicious harm: up to $250,000 / 10 years

## OCR Investigation Triggers
- Complaints
- Breach reports
- Media reports
- CMS referrals
- Random audits

## Compliance Logic
- Document all corrective actions with timestamps
- Demonstrate good faith effort to comply
- Maintain evidence of training, policies, and risk analysis
